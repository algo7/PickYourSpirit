# PickYourSpirit

Wanted a wolf and got a moose again? Equip the **Spirit Caller** and press **middle mouse** to pick who answers your next
call: bear, moose, wolf, boar, or random like the unmodded game. Nothing to install on the server.

## How to Use

1. Equip the Spirit Caller.
2. Press **middle mouse** (secondary attack button). The middle of the screen shows who's next, and
   the key hints show **Next spirit**. Keep pressing to go through them all and back to random.
3. Cast as usual.

## Same Rules as the Game

You can have as many of each kind as your staff's level. Call one more and your oldest of that kind goes home. With a
level 1 staff that's one of each.

## Multiplayer

Only you need the mod. Your spirits are the game's own, so friends without the mod see them as usual, and their own staff
stays random.

## Compatibility

- Built and tested for Valheim 1.0 (Deep North), on Linux; no OS-specific code.
- Middle mouse picks spirits only while the Spirit Caller is equipped; your other weapons work as before.
- The mod's own words are in English; spirit names follow your game language.
- It only changes the Spirit Caller, so it shouldn't clash with other mods, except ones that also change what the staff
  summons.

## Uninstalling

Nothing is saved, so just remove it.
